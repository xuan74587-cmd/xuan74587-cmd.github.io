I am **Jade (周璇)**, a master's student in Software Engineering at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Wuhan University of Technology. I studied Network Engineering at the School of Computer Science, Yangtze University as an undergraduate.

My research focuses on **AIOps and multimodal root cause analysis for microservices**, including cross-system diagnosis and LLM/agent-assisted log understanding and fault reasoning. I also work on intelligent data processing and automated workflows for industrial and logistics applications.

<h2 id="research">Research interests</h2>

<ol class="research-list">
  <li><h3>Microservice root cause analysis</h3><p>Fault propagation and root cause localization in microservices, with an emphasis on interpretable diagnosis.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Multimodal diagnosis and cross-domain generalization</h3><p>Combining logs, metrics, and traces for cross-system fault diagnosis with limited labels.</p></li>
  <li><h3>LLM/agent-based intelligent diagnosis</h3><p>Exploring LLMs and agents for log understanding, fault reasoning, and coordinated tool use.</p></li>
  <li><h3>AI systems engineering and industry applications</h3><p>Intelligent data processing, workflow orchestration, and reliable systems for industry and logistics.</p></li>
</ol>

<h2 id="projects">Current research and projects</h2>

### Multimodal root cause analysis for microservices
Exploratory research
{: .project-meta}

Investigating joint diagnosis using logs, metrics, and traces, with a focus on cross-system generalization and fine-grained root cause localization. Reviewing approaches to multimodal fusion and fault propagation modeling.

### LLM/agent-assisted log analysis
Exploratory research
{: .project-meta}

Studying LLMs and agents for log parsing, anomaly understanding, and root cause reasoning. Exploring multi-step reasoning and tool collaboration, with attention to diagnostic evidence and interpretability.

### Industrial log analysis and intelligent diagnosis
Application-oriented investigation
{: .project-meta}

Investigating log parsing, anomaly detection, and fault localization for industrial metering automation systems, aiming to identify diagnosis approaches suited to real operational needs.

### Intelligent logistics processing platform
Industry application · Engineering development
{: .project-meta}

Contributing to a port logistics platform built on **LLMs, agents, and intelligent workflows** for email classification, field extraction, domain knowledge mapping, quality checks, and multiple EDI message formats. Combining human review and automated testing in an end-to-end process from unstructured input to standardized messages, with efficiency and reliability as engineering goals.

### Research experimentation and model evaluation
Research infrastructure · Engineering development
{: .project-meta}

Building a reproducible experimentation workspace for configuration management, run tracking, metric analysis, and result comparison to support algorithm research and model evaluation.

## Previous projects

### Aelos robot vision and activation
Core developer · Mar 2025–Aug 2025
{: .project-meta}

Implemented object recognition, tracking, and motion control using **OpenCV and lightweight deep learning models**, and optimized image preprocessing and serial communication. The project received a **National First Prize at the 27th China Robot and Artificial Intelligence Competition**.

### OCR- and AI-based personnel archive retrieval
Project lead · Started Oct 2024
{: .project-meta}

Designed a multilingual OCR model combining ResNet, LSTM, and attention. Developed retrieval, structured storage, and access control modules using inverted indexing, fuzzy matching, and MySQL. The project was **recommended at the university level**.

### Smart seat
Core developer · Mar 2023–Apr 2024
{: .project-meta}

Led the system design, collected pressure data using ESP8266 and sensors, and developed Python data cleaning and inference modules. Independently built an HTML/CSS/JavaScript dashboard for posture monitoring, fatigue detection, and multi-user data visualization.
